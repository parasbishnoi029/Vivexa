# Vivexa User Guide

## Registration
1. Navigate to the Vivexa homepage.
2. Click **Get Started**.
3. Fill in your details (First Name, Last Name, Company, Email, Password).
4. Click **Create account**.
5. Check your email for a verification link to confirm your account.

## Login
1. Click **Log in** on the homepage.
2. Enter your email and password.
3. Click **Sign in**.

## Dashboard
The main Dashboard gives you an overview of your workspaces, including total projects, datasets, and storage. It is continuously updated from your actual usage data.

## Projects
- **Create**: Navigate to Projects, click "New Project", and fill out the wizard.
- **Rename/Edit**: Hover over a project card and click the Edit icon.
- **Archive**: Click the Archive icon on a project card to toggle its status.
- **Delete**: Click the Trash icon to permanently remove a project.

## Dataset Upload
1. Navigate to **Datasets**.
2. Click **Upload Dataset**.
3. Select your CSV, Excel, JSON, or Parquet file.
4. The system validates and securely uploads your data to the cloud storage bucket.

## Dataset Analysis & AI Analyst
- Navigate to the **Dataset Detail** page to preview your data.
- Click **Analyze with AI** to open the AI Chat.
- The AI acts as a Senior Data Scientist. Ask questions to get Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), business insights, and more.
- The AI will provide evidence, confidence scores, and recommendations.

## Settings & Profile
- Access settings via the Profile dropdown in the top right.
- Update your avatar, email, and full name.

## Plans
- Manage your subscription by clicking **Billing** in the Profile dropdown.
- Currently, **Free** is standard. Contact the Admin for **Pro** or **Enterprise**.

## Admin (For Administrators Only)
- A separate interface to view global platform metrics like Total Users and System Health.
- Only authorized administrator emails (e.g., parasbishnoi012@gmail.com) have access.
