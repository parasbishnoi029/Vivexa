# AI Data Analytics Platform

## AI Reasoning Engine (AIRE)

Version: 1.0

Priority: Critical

---

# 1. Purpose

The AI Reasoning Engine (AIRE) is responsible for converting verified analytical outputs into professional business intelligence.

Unlike traditional chatbots, AIRE never performs calculations directly.

Its role is to:

- Understand user intent
- Decide which analytics modules to execute
- Explain results
- Generate recommendations
- Produce executive reports

# 4. AI Orchestrator

The Orchestrator controls every AI request.

Responsibilities

Understand request

Select required modules

Execute workflow

Combine results

Generate final answer

Never skip validation.

---

# 5. AI Specialists

The system is designed around specialist roles.

Data Engineer

Prepares datasets.

Statistician

Chooses statistical methods.

ML Engineer

Runs predictive models.

Business Analyst

Explains business impact.

Visualization Expert

Chooses best charts.

Report Writer

Creates professional reports.

QA Reviewer

Checks consistency before response.

Initially all roles may be handled by one model.
Future versions may use independent agents.
